Export to Fabric

Export to Fabric publishes a model's Report as a Fabric app. People open it in the browser after signing in to Fabric, and use its tiles and slicers there with nothing to install.

It is experimental, and it will change

Export to Fabric is an experiment. It works today, but it is not finished, and you should not build on it yet.

  • It is built on Fabric apps, which is new and still changing. When Fabric apps changes, the export and the apps it makes change with it.
  • What the export asks, what the app looks like, the .fabricapp file, and how the app signs in and reads its data can all change from one version of OneLake Studio to the next.
  • An app you publish today may need to be exported again after an update, and may stop working until you do.
  • Numbers computed from a semantic model aren't guaranteed to match Power BI.

The rest of OneLake Studio only reads. Export to Fabric is one of the two features that write, with Upload Files, and it asks before it writes anything.

What it publishes

The export publishes the Report page of a model, as it is, with the Fabric REST API. Only people who can open the app in Fabric can load it.

The app is also saved on your computer as one .fabricapp file. Double-click it to open it again in OneLake Studio, on its Report page.
